STYLE: Codex digital pet style adapted for anime/game character. Pixel-art-adjacent chibi mascot: oversized head, tiny body, large simplified eyes that keep the character's iconic eye color and shape. 1-2 px dark outlines, visible pixel edges, no soft antialiasing. Limited palette taken from the character's signature colors (4-6 tones). Flat cel shading. Keep one or two distinctive features (hair shape, hair color, signature accessory) but radically simplify everything else. The result must read as a chibi pet, not as anime keyart, not as a 3D render, not as a polished portrait.
