STYLE: Chibi cute platformer style. Super deformed proportions (2-3 heads tall), big heads, small bodies, stubby limbs. Adorable and endearing. Soft rounded outlines. Pastel or bright candy colors. Expressive faces dominate the character. Cute accessories (bow, cape, hat). Character should feel like Kirby, Diddy Kong, or the Mario characters - friendly and approachable heroes.

Movement in this style can be slightly bouncy and exaggerated - cuteness over realism.
