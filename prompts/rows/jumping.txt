ACTION: jumping - the pet performs one full jump, anticipation through landing.

8-FRAME BREAKDOWN:
Frame 0: neutral standing.
Frame 1: anticipation - pet crouches, knees bent, body lowered.
Frame 2: deepest crouch, body very compressed.
Frame 3: launch - legs extending, body rising, feet just leaving the ground.
Frame 4: peak of jump - body at highest point, legs tucked or trailing, often a little arch in the back, joyful expression.
Frame 5: descent - falling, body coming down, legs preparing to land.
Frame 6: landing - feet contact, body absorbing impact, knees bent.
Frame 7: settle - returning to neutral standing.

Show jump entirely through body position (vertical change). The pet's vertical position should clearly differ across frames - especially frame 4 should be visibly higher than frame 0.

ROW-SPECIFIC FORBIDDEN: no shadow on the floor, no dust at the feet, no impact bursts, no landing star/burst symbols, no bounce-pad lines, no floor cue marks, no motion lines around the body, no afterimages of the previous position.
