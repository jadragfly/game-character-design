ACTION: attack - the character performs a punch/strike attack.

8-FRAME BREAKDOWN:
Frame 0: ready stance - body in fighting position, fist pulled back, body coiled for power.
Frame 1: wind-up - arm beginning to extend backward, body rotating slightly away from attack direction.
Frame 2: strike launch - arm thrusting forward rapidly, body rotating toward attack direction.
Frame 3: impact - arm fully extended, body at maximum extension, fist at peak speed.
Frame 4: follow-through - arm continuing past impact, body momentum carrying forward.
Frame 5: recovery begin - arm starting to retract, body beginning to return to stance.
Frame 6: mid-recovery - arm half retracted, body returning to ready position.
Frame 7: back to ready - fist pulled back, body in original stance, ready for next attack.

CHARACTER DIRECTION: Character faces [RIGHT/LEFT] and attacks in that direction.

ATTACK STYLE OPTIONS:
- punch: closed fist, arm extended
- slash: open hand or sword, arc motion
- strike: palm or heel, downward motion

GAME CONTEXT: This attack is quick and decisive - fits a nimble platformer character. The attack should be readable in a single frame (frame 3 or 4).

FORBIDDEN: No weapon trails. No impact stars or bursts on the character. No wind effects. The attack motion must be clean and readable.
