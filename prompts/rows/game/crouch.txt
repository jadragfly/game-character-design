ACTION: crouch - the character crouches down into a defensive or ready position.

8-FRAME BREAKDOWN:
Frame 0: standing - normal standing pose, full height.
Frame 1: beginning to crouch - knees bending, body lowering, arms beginning to move.
Frame 2: mid-crouch - body significantly lowered, knees deeply bent, arms positioned for balance or holding.
Frame 3: full crouch - body at lowest point, knees at maximum bend, body compressed, looking alert.

CRITICAL: The character height in frame 3 should be visibly lower than frame 0 - typically 50-70% of standing height.

CHARACTER DIRECTION: Character faces [RIGHT/LEFT] consistently throughout.

GAME CONTEXT: This crouch is used in platformers for:
- Ducking under obstacles
- Preparing for a ground attack
- Taking cover
- Charging power for special moves

The character should look COORDINATED and READY, not like they fell down. The head stays up, eyes alert.

FORBIDDEN: No sliding motion. No looking tired or exhausted. The character is in a READY stance, not resting.
