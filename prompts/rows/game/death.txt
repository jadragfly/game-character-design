ACTION: death - the character is defeated.

8-FRAME BREAKDOWN:
Frame 0: before death - character standing, unaware or facing final blow.
Frame 1: final hit - impact, body jolts backward.
Frame 2: collapse start - legs giving out, body beginning to fall backward.
Frame 3: falling - body fully airborne, tumbling backward.
Frame 4: impact - back hits ground, slight bounce or ragdoll.
Frame 5: on ground - body lying flat, motionless.
Frame 6: fading - optional: character sprite becoming transparent or fading out.
Frame 7: gone - character off-screen or faded.

ALTERNATIVE DEATH STYLES:

Style A - Quick defeat:
Frame 0-3: hit reaction
Frame 4-7: quick fade out, character stays where they fall

Style B - Dramatic fall:
More frames of tumbling, arms flailing during fall, larger bounce on impact.

CHARACTER DIRECTION: Original facing direction (death typically happens in place).

GAME CONTEXT: Death animations in platformers are usually brief. The character respawns quickly, so this animation shouldn't be too dramatic. Quick and readable is best.

FORBIDDEN: No blood. No gore. No disturbing imagery. This is a family-friendly platformer. Keep it light - the character simply gets defeated and fades.

EXPRESSION: Frames 4-7 should show minimal expression - the character is simply no longer active. Not pain, not sorrow, just... not there anymore.
