ACTION: hit - the character takes damage and reacts.

8-FRAME BREAKDOWN:
Frame 0: before impact - character in neutral state, unaware of incoming hit.
Frame 1: impact moment - hit connects, body jolts in opposite direction, brief flash or stagger.
Frame 2: recoil - body pushed back from impact, legs staggering, arms thrown up for defense.
Frame 3: maximum recoil - body at furthest point from impact direction, stumbling.
Frame 4: recovery start - body beginning to stabilize, legs trying to regain balance.
Frame 5: mid recovery - standing more upright, still dazed.
Frame 6: near recovery - almost back to normal stance, eyes refocusing.
Frame 7: recovered - standing again, ready for action, may have different expression (determined/pain).

CHARACTER DIRECTION: Character faces [RIGHT/LEFT]. The hit comes from the OPPOSITE direction.

GAME CONTEXT: This is a damage reaction - the character is resilient and keeps fighting. Not overly dramatic or slow. Quick recovery is important for gameplay.

EXPRESSION CHANGE: By frame 7, the character should look DETERMINED or ANGRY, not sad or defeated. This character doesn't give up easily.

FORBIDDEN: No stars circling head. No exaggerated tears. No crying. No prolonged agony. The character recovers quickly and stays combat-ready.
